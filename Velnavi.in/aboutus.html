Our Story

We believe speaking is the fastest way to learn

Velnavi started with a simple observation - people can read and write English just fine, but freeze up the moment they have to speak it. We built Velnavi to fix exactly that.

Why We Started

A problem we lived ourselves

Like thousands of students across India, our founder struggled to speak confidently in English during placement interviews - not because of a lack of knowledge, but because of a lack of practice. Grammar books and vocabulary apps didn't help. What actually helped was talking to real people, again and again, until the fear went away.

That's the entire idea behind Velnavi - connect real people who want to practice English, give them a comfortable space to talk, and let consistent conversation do what no course ever could.

🎯

Our Mission

To give every learner - especially students preparing for interviews and placements - real speaking practice, not just more grammar drills. Confidence comes from conversation, not memorization.

💡

Our Vision

A world where no one misses an opportunity - a job, a friendship, a conversation - simply because they weren't confident speaking English. Velnavi wants to be the place where that confidence is built.

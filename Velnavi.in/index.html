How It Works

What is Velnavi?

Velnavi is a platform that helps you become a confident English speaker by connecting you directly with real people for live conversations. No boring lessons, no grammar drills - just genuine practice that builds real speaking confidence over time.

1
Create Your Profile
Start by telling us a little about yourself - your native language, your current English level, and the topics you're most interested in practicing. This helps us understand exactly what kind of conversations will help you improve the fastest.
2
Find a Partner
Once you're ready, Velnavi matches you with a real person during our daily practice window. Our matching considers your level and interests, so you always end up talking to someone who's a good fit for a meaningful conversation.
3
Start Talking
Jump into a focused 20-minute voice conversation with your matched partner. There's no pressure to be perfect - the goal is simply to speak, listen, and get comfortable expressing yourself in English, just like a real conversation with a friend.
4
Track Your Growth
After every conversation, Velnavi keeps track of your progress - how many sessions you've completed, total minutes practiced, and your daily streak. Watching these numbers grow is a great way to stay motivated and see how far you've come.

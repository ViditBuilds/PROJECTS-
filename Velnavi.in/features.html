Everything you need to speak English confidently

Velnavi is built around one simple idea - the fastest way to improve your spoken English is by actually speaking it, with real people, in real conversations. Here's everything Velnavi offers to make that happen.

🎤

Voice Conversations

Talk to real people through 20-minute voice calls - no typing, no text chat. This is where real speaking confidence is built.
